Rx Pharmacy Courier is an experienced prescription delivery service. This service helps pharmacies to enhance their service to their customers. Our service will bridge the gap between you and your customer in a cost-effective manner, and the service is quick and effective.


Rx Pharmacy Courier Service is a United States base company, which originated
in 1998 and managed by a team of health care professionals. At the time of establishment their was no prescription delivery service from the store to your customer’s front door. The company started the delivery service in the Southern Region delivering to Pharmacy’s in the communities. In June 2003, the company have expanded there service into the Western Region. Providing service to business, home, medical facilities, pharmacy and pharmaceutical company. Pharmacy Courier Service became a Limited Liability Company, which allowed the company to expanded services with in the United States.
